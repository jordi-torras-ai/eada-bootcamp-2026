HOMEPRICE MARKET — OPEN WITHOUT INSTALLATION

Double-click index.html. The main price estimator works immediately, with Ames sale-price model and FHFA 2026 Q2 metro index adjustment.

This zero-setup edition uses local browser storage for users, listings and messages. It is a demonstration on one browser only: information DOES NOT sync to other browsers, devices, or other buyers and sellers. Do not use real passwords or sensitive contact information. The app is not hosted.

For a real marketplace shared between people, the accompanying original SQLite/Python app must be deployed to an internet server. The one-click edition is NOT SQLite and cannot replace secure server-based authentication.
